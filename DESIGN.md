## Brand & Style

This design system embodies soulful slow travel and grounded eco-luxury, shaped by the coastal forest rhythms of Abéné, Casamance. The aesthetic moves away from sterile resort minimalism toward organic African warmth, tactile editorial storytelling, and raw craftsmanship.

Every interface element mirrors unhurried hospitality and immersion in natural elements—woven thatch, rammed earth, sea breezes, and shade beneath ancient trees. The target audience seeks mindful retreat, cultural resonance, and refined comfort without ostentation. Visual hierarchy favors generous negative space, gentle transitions, warm natural sunlight, and structured editorial framing that honors the sanctuary of the Atlantic Casamance coast.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural geography of Casamance: red laterite earth, baobab groves, sun-washed sand, and deep forest shadows.

- **Primary (`#B85D3B` - Laterite Terracotta):** Reserved for primary conversion actions, high-intent booking states, active markers, and focal editorial badges.
- **Secondary (`#3F5443` - Baobab Olive Green):** Applied to ecological callouts, conservation metrics, subtle verification status pills, and botanical accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C4B39A` - Warm Stone):** Utilized for structural borders, hairline grid divisions, interactive element outlines, and subtle container strokes.
- **Neutral (`#382112` - Deep Espresso Brown):** Primary typographic color, replacing harsh pitch-black with deep organic warmth and high-contrast legibility.

### Surface Tones & Secondary Text
- **Canvas Base:** Raw Linen (`#F7F2EA`) provides the open canvas background.
- **Surface Elevation / Cards:** Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`) provides subtle background grounding for cards, sidebars, and grouped content.
- **Secondary Body & Metadata:** Muted Bark Brown (`#735C49`) handles auxiliary details, captions, and secondary descriptions.

## Typography

The type system blends literary elegance with clean, functional modern legibility. 

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used across hero banners, sanctuary and suite naming, experiential chapter headings, and key quotes. Its classical proportions and soft serifs lend historical grounding and quiet poise.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Light (`300`) and Regular (`400`) weights set at an airy 1.65 line-height ratio create effortless reading across long-form itinerary and lodge descriptions.
- **Eyebrows & Navigation:** Rendered exclusively in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with deliberate kerning (`letterSpacing: 0.15em` to `0.2em`) to introduce architectural rhythm and modern restraint above expressive serif headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial fluid-grid foundation, allowing imagery and text to breathe unhindered:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 56px (`3.5rem`) outer canvas margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Content blocks routinely employ asymmetric offset placements (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for adjacent prose) to emulate a luxury travel monograph.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) margins and 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Stacking transitions smoothly to single-column vertical stories without compromising vertical breathing room.

Horizontal padding inside modules and section transitions utilizes the expanded vertical rhythm of `space-xl` (64px) to preserve a spacious, restorative experience.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain authentic tactile simplicity, artificial drop shadows are strictly avoided. Depth is achieved entirely through organic tonal layering and delicate borders:

- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas rests on Raw Linen (`#F7F2EA`). Elevated modular components (lodging cards, booking drawers, amenity spotlights) sit on Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`). Overlays and floating navigation employ a high-opacity tint of Raw Linen (`rgba(247, 242, 234, 0.94)`) combined with a soft 12px blur backdrop.
- **Low-Contrast Framing:** Spatial boundaries are demarcated by 1px solid Warm Stone borders (`#C4B39A`) rendered at full opacity or softened to 40% opacity for internal dividing rules.
- **Tactile Hover States:** Interactive elements indicate depth on hover through subtle inward fills or tone shifts—shifting from Warm Sand to a tint of Raw Linen—rather than raising outward with dropshadows.

## Shapes

The architectural envelope relies on soft, understated geometry (`roundedness: 1` — 4px / `0.25rem` standard radii, extending to 8px / `0.5rem` for expansive image containers and cards). 

This deliberate geometric discipline reflects handcrafted earth bricks, timber lintels, and framed woven mats. Overly rounded bubbles or circular capsules are avoided, except for organic tag badges which use subtle pill contours to contrast with the formal architectural grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Laterite Terracotta):** Background `#B85D3B`, typography in uppercase off-white (`#F7F2EA`), 4px border radius. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover shifts tone slightly deeper with an organic transition (`#A24F30`).
- **Secondary Action (Warm Stone Outline):** Transparent background with a 1px `#C4B39A` border and `#382112` text. Hover transitions to Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`) background fill.
- **Text Button / Editorial Links:** Deep Espresso typography followed by a persistent 1px hairline rule beneath, expanding gently outward on focus.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`) or transparent on Raw Linen canvas.
- Border: 1px hairline Warm Stone (`#C4B39A`), turning to Laterite Terracotta (`#B85D3B`) upon active focus.
- Labels: Set in uppercase letter-spaced `label-eyebrow` styling placed above the input field. Placeholder text is set in Muted Bark Brown (`#735C49`).

### Badges & Organic Chips
- Ecology and sustainability pills utilize a pale Baobab tint background with solid Baobab Olive Green (`#3F5443`) text, enclosed with a light 1px stroke.
- Spacing: `4px 12px` with full pill radius for organic distinction against rectilinear architectural modules.

### Cards & Sanctuary Showcases
- Surface: Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`) framed with a 1px `#C4B39A` outline.
- Layout: Generous interior padding (`space-lg`), pairing full-bleed or bordered imagery with an eyebrow tag, serif title in Deep Espresso (`#382112`), and description in Muted Bark Brown (`#735C49`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box dimensions: 18x18px with 2px corner radius for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Border: 1.5px Warm Stone (`#C4B39A`).
- Active State: Filled with Baobab Olive Green (`#3F5443`) or Laterite Terracotta (`#B85D3B`) featuring an off-white inner checkmark.

### Dividers & Flourishes
- Dividers are single-pixel continuous lines in Warm Stone (`#C4B39A`), used purposefully to partition narrative chapters and technical reservation breakdowns.
